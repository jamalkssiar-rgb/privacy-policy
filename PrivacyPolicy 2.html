Privacy Policy for Cute Piano Game

Last updated: May 2026

Mellifluous Studios ("we", "us", or "our") built the Cute Piano Game app as a free-to-play mobile game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Non-Personal Data

We collect non-personal data automatically when you use our app, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App usage statistics (levels played, time spent, scores)
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID)
  • Crash reports and performance data

Personal Data

We do not require you to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Local Game Data

All game progress data including achievements, level progress, coins, diamonds, and high scores are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It includes:

  • Coins and diamonds balance
  • Unlocked songs and levels
  • Achievement progress
  • High scores and stars
  • Game settings and preferences

This data can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data from your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:
